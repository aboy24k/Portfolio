Servo commands, last 8 seconds

Each line is one channel after smoothing and the speed limit.

Each channel drives one servo. Switch a channel off if your arm doesn't have that joint; it will hold its rest angle. Open a channel to change its ranges or reverse it.

Arm firmware

This sketch runs on an Arduino or ESP32 wired to a PCA9685 servo board. It clamps every angle to limits you set, limits speed, and holds position if the computer stops sending.


    

    

      
Serial protocol

      

        S,a0,…,a10Target angles in degrees, 0 to 180, one per channel. Sent at the update rate.
        RRelease every servo (limp).
        HHold: resume at the last position.
        PPing. The board answers PONG.
      

      
Channels 0 to 4 are thumb, index, middle, ring, pinky. 5 to 7 are wrist roll, pitch, yaw. 8 to 10 are arm swing, lift, reach. Channels you switch off are sent at their rest angle.
