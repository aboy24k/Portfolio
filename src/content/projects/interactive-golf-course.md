## What I built

An interactive electromechanical golf course that took second place.

- **Automation.** Automated door features and dynamic timing mechanisms, controlled by Arduino.
- **Motion.** Electromechanical actuators handle the movement.
- **Fabrication.** Designed in AutoCAD, with parts 3D printed and machined in the shop.
